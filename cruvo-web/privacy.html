PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Welcome to CRUVO (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application and related backend services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of the App, and from third-party services.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Registration Information: When you create an account or sign in via Google, we collect your Username, Email Address, and Display Name.
  • Rider Profile Details: You may choose to complete a rider profile, providing optional details such as Bio, Phone Number, Location City, Motorcycle details (Make/Model), Riding Characteristics, and Profile Photo.
  • Ride Metadata: Details of rides you schedule or join, including ride names, dates, times, and roles.

B. Location and Tracking Data

Because CRUVO is a squad riding coordination utility, we collect precise location data:

  • Continuous Real-Time GPS Tracking: When you participate in an Active Ride, we collect your Latitude, Longitude, Heading, and Speed.
  • Route & Navigation Paths: We store start locations, destinations, checkpoints, and route lines.
  • Incident and Stop Flags: Coordinates and categories of stops flagged during trips.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication & Security: To verify user accounts, log you in securely, and handle password reset OTPs.
  • Real-Time Squad Coordination: To stream your live position to other riders in your active ride.
  • Route Calculation: To compute route distances and travel times using mapping services (TomTom API).

3. How Your Information Is Shared

  • With Other Riders: Display name, profile details, and active ride GPS position are visible to participants of your ride.
  • Third-Party Services: Google API Services (OAuth login & transactional email), TomTom API (mapping), and Database Hosting (PostgreSQL).
