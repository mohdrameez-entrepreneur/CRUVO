## Brand & Style

**CRUVO Minimal (Off-White, Navy Blue & Amber Yellow)** is a 3-color minimalist design system built for maximum outdoor legibility and effortless tactile response:
- **Base Color (Very Off-White `#F8F9FA`):** Warm, soft light base that eliminates screen glare under direct sunlight.
- **Structural Color (Midnight Navy `#0A192F`):** Deep, authoritative navy blue used for headings, primary text, and clean framing.
- **Accent Color (Amber Yellow `#F59E0B`):** High-visibility punch used for primary CTAs, active pills, and status alerts.
- **Complimentary Utility (Ice Blue `#38BDF8`):** Reserved exclusively for live telemetry readings and location radar pulses.

## Typography (Apple Human Interface Inspired)

- **Inter (Medium 500 & SemiBold 600):** Clean geometric headings and titles without aggressive extra-bold weights.
- **Inter (Regular 400):** Description text, metadata, and form values.
- **JetBrains Mono (Medium 500):** Speed, distance, coordinates, and timestamp telemetry.

## Glassmorphism & Elevation

- Light frosted glass panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) with subtle hairline outlines (`rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.08)`).
- Soft ambient shadows (`rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.1)`) replacing harsh dark-mode glows.
